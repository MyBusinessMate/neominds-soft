## **1\. Client Context**

Businesses receive potential customer enquiries through multiple digital channels. Converting these enquiries into actionable sales opportunities requires collecting information, organizing leads, and ensuring that relevant enquiries reach the appropriate teams.

The project focused on automating the lead capture process through an integrated workflow, reducing repetitive administrative work and improving the flow of incoming lead information.

## **2\. The Challenge**

Manual lead management can create delays between receiving an enquiry and taking the next sales action.

Key challenges included:

* **Scattered Lead Sources:** Incoming enquiries can originate from different digital channels.  
* **Manual Data Collection:** Teams may need to repeatedly collect and enter lead information.  
* **Lead Qualification:** Identifying relevant prospects requires reviewing submitted information.  
* **Delayed Follow-ups:** Leads can be missed or contacted late when follow-up depends on manual processes.  
* **Information Organization:** Inconsistent lead records can make sales coordination difficult.

## **3\. Project Constraints**

The automation needed to connect existing lead sources and business applications without unnecessarily changing established operations.

Key considerations included:

* Integrating incoming lead sources through available APIs and webhooks.  
* Processing lead information in a structured format.  
* Supporting AI-assisted lead qualification.  
* Preventing duplicate or incomplete lead records where applicable.  
* Coordinating notifications and downstream actions.  
* Handling failed integrations and workflow exceptions.

**3\. AI-Assisted Lead Qualification**

Integrated AI capabilities to support the interpretation and qualification of lead information.

**4\. Lead Routing**

Organized the workflow around directing processed lead information to the appropriate connected systems or teams.

**5\. Follow-up Automation**

Established an integration foundation for triggering relevant notifications and follow-up activities.
