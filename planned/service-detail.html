Frequently Asked Questions

Technical & Delivery Inquiries

Common engineering and business questions from our enterprise clients.

Can your AI systems integrate with our legacy on-premise ERP or database? ▼
Yes. We build custom reverse proxies, secure VPN relays, and CDC (Change Data Capture) connectors that integrate with legacy ERPs like SAP ECC/S4, Epicor Prophet 21, AS400, and NetSuite without requiring risky overhaul of your core transaction database.
How do you guarantee against model hallucination in financial/transactional flows? ▼
We enforce deterministic validation frameworks. LLM agents do not write directly to databases; they generate structured schema payloads (JSON Schema / Pydantic) that must pass automated unit constraints, reference checks against ERP ledgers, and confidence threshold gates before execution.
What are the typical project timelines from architecture to production? ▼
Phase 1 (Architecture & Pilot Sandbox) ships in 2 to 3 weeks. Phase 2 (Enterprise staging with full ERP integration) takes 4 to 6 weeks. Full production deployment with automated monitoring and load testing typically concludes within 8 to 12 weeks.
Who owns the IP and custom code developed during the engagement? ▼
You own 100% of all intellectual property, custom models, training pipelines, and codebase from day one. Neominds works strictly on a work-for-hire basis with full mutual NDA protection.
