Initial State & Bottlenecks

The Challenge: Drowning in Manual Quotes & Catalog Complexity

Apex Beverage manufactures mission-critical commercial dispensing systems with tens of thousands of replacement gaskets, valves, and pressurized fittings. Before the engagement:

01

Zero Digital Self-Service

100% of orders were received via phone, fax, and emailed PDF order sheets. The sales team was trapped acting as manual order takers instead of building distributor accounts.

02

Exploded Assembly Parts Identification

Field technicians had to manually cross-reference 400-page paper schematic manuals to locate a single sub-component serial number, causing high return rates and wrong-part orders.

03

Contracted Tiered Pricing In ERP

Every commercial buyer has custom contracted price schedules, credit terms (Net 30/60), and freight allowances locked inside on-premise SAP ERP that standard ecommerce couldn't reflect.

Engineering Execution

The Solution: Real-Time Middleware & Visual Exploded Commerce

Neominds engineered a robust headless architecture integrating the commerce frontend with Apex Beverage's SAP backend.

Multi-Tier Data Flow Blueprint

1
Interactive Exploded Schematic SVG Canvas:

Engineers can pinch-to-zoom into interactive 3D/SVG equipment blueprints, click directly on any valve or gasket hot-spot, view instant inventory, and add to cart in 1 click.

2
Sub-Second Real-Time SAP Price & Inventory Middleware:

Microservices cache distributor-specific pricing contracts with Redis edge nodes while validating credit lines against SAP before order dispatch.

3
Automated Bulk B2B Ordering & CSV Re-Order Engine:

Enables wholesale partners to upload spreadsheets containing 500+ SKUs with instant SKU validation, replacement suggestions for discontinued items, and automated PO generation.
