The 3 Critical Bottlenecks in Modern Healthcare Tech

Why off-the-shelf software consistently fails healthcare providers and patient care teams.

01

EHR Silos & Data Fragmentation

Critical patient histories remain trapped in legacy EHR formats. Without high-speed FHIR normalization, clinicians spend 16 minutes per patient simply piecing together medication records.

02

Compliance vs. Velocity Dilemma

Generic cloud developers build apps that fail HIPAA audits, expose ePHI in unencrypted caches, or lack mandatory cryptographic access logging required by federal regulators.

03

Patient Engagement Friction

Clunky web portals suffer from 65%+ patient drop-off rates. Patients demand mobile-first, biometrically authenticated apps with asynchronous chat and instant prescription renewals.
